How the Computer Solve Works

The app contains a precomputed optimal-policy database covering all 933,120 reachable Pyraminx main-body states. For each state, one compact byte records its exact distance from solved and the best next move along a shortest path. The embedded database is run-length compressed and expands into the direct lookup table when first needed.

When solving a scramble, the app directly indexes the current edge-and-center state, reads the optimal next move, applies it, and repeats the lookup until solved. No live state-space search is needed. Because every lookup follows a distance exactly one step closer to solved, the resulting main-body move list is guaranteed shortest.

With the current Computer Solve method, the main body—edges and centers—can be solved in at most 11 moves. The four tips are independent and may require up to one move each, giving an overall worst case of 15 moves.
