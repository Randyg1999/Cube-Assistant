Megaminx Phase 1
================

Implemented:
- Regular dodecahedral geometry derived from the dual icosahedron.
- 12 centers, 30 physical edge pieces, 20 physical corner pieces.
- 12 independently turnable faces at 72 degrees.
- Prime and double turns for every face.
- Sticker-drag face turning using screen-space tangent direction.
- Orbit camera remains available when dragging the background.
- 40-move scramble and reset.
- Entirely local/offline; no new dependency.

Architecture:
- js/megaminx-engine.js              logical geometry and move engine
- js/megaminx/render-megaminx.js     dodecahedron/sticker renderer and animation
- js/megaminx/controls-megaminx.js   manual buttons, drag controls, scramble/reset

Phase 1 deliberately does NOT include manual sticker entry, reachability verification,
solver logic, or camera scanning. Those belong to later phases.
