4x4 Center Solver — Ordered Human-Style Bar Planner
====================================================

This build makes center solving deterministic in both method and presentation.

Center order
------------
1. Yellow — top
2. White — bottom
3. Green — front
4. Blue — back
5. Orange — right
6. Red — left

Planner
-------
- Uses short wide-turn center-building triggers such as Rw U Rw', Rw U2 Rw',
  and rotated/mirrored equivalents.
- Builds and joins 1x2 bars while protecting previously solved center blocks.
- Plans from the current state; it does not reverse scramble history.
- The prior human-style global planner and deterministic U2 solver remain as
  safety fallbacks if the ordered planner cannot find a route.

Guided descriptions
-------------------
Each move is tagged with the planner's actual current intent. Examples include:
- Building first Yellow bar
- Building second Yellow bar
- Matching both yellow bars to complete the yellow center
- Moving the white work bar out of the way before restoring the yellow center
- Positioning the second green bar for the front center
- Matching both blue bars while preserving the solved centers

Playback
--------
Compute Centers, Next Move, Back, Auto-Play/Pause, and speed control are all
available in the 4x4 Guided Solve panel.

Validation performed for this build
-----------------------------------
10 independent random 40-move 4x4 scrambles after the ordered/annotation change:
- 10/10 center solves verified
- 10/10 used the ordered human-style planner
- 0 U2 fallbacks
- average 39.1 displayed moves
- shortest 34 moves
- longest 44 moves

Displayed move count uses the app's normal notation, where a wide move such as
Rw is one move.

The legacy U2 fallback strategy is adapted from Cameron LaBounty's MIT-licensed
Rubiks-Cube-Solver; see THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt.
